Our Journey in Bamenda

From a small idea to a community-changing platform, see how AfriMart has grown.

2022

Bamenda Community Challenge

AfriMart was selected as a winning project in the Bamenda Community Challenge, receiving initial funding and mentorship to develop the platform.

Jan 2023

First 50 Vendors Onboard

We launched our pilot program with 50 local vendors from across Bamenda, testing the platform with real users and gathering feedback.

Jun 2023

Public Launch

AfriMart opened to all Bamenda vendors, with special training sessions to help local businesses get online.

Today

Growing Together

With over 125 vendors and thousands of products, AfriMart continues to expand while staying true to our mission of empowering local commerce.
